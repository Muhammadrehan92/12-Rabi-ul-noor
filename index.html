Rahmatul-lil-Alameen

The Significance of 12 Rabi-ul-Noor ﷺ

The arrival of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ brought light, justice, love, and guidance to all of humanity.

Mercy to All Creation

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was sent as a mercy not only for Muslims, but for all human beings, animals, and existence.

Perfect Character (Sunnah)

His life serves as the highest moral standard of honesty, humility, generosity, forgiveness, and wisdom.

Peace & Brotherhood

He united tribes, abolished racial discrimination, protected the weak, and established universal brotherhood.
