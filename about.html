History & Meaning

Why We Celebrate 12 Rabi-ul-Noor ﷺ

The month of Rabi-ul-Awwal (also cherished as Rabi-ul-Noor, the Spring of Light) holds a sacred position in Islamic history. On the 12th of Rabi-ul-Awwal in the year of the Elephant (570 CE), Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born in the blessed city of Makkah.

His birth transformed the world, removing ignorance and injustice while replacing them with knowledge, moral excellence, and devotion to the One Creator.
